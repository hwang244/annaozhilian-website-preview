OUR TEAM

Clinical expertise and technology.
One rehabilitation purpose.

AnnoBrain’s core team brings together professionals from rehabilitation departments at tertiary hospitals, AI laboratories and medical technology companies. We combine clinical experience, algorithm research and product engineering in one multidisciplinary team.

Our expertise spans artificial intelligence, computer vision, large model development and game engine architecture. Together, these capabilities support AI assessment, game-based training and expert digital twins, connecting rehabilitation from clinic to home.
